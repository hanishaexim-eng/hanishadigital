How fast is an MVP?

How do I keep the site fast on Vercel (LCP)?

Prefer WebP/AVIF for images, always set width and height to avoid layout shift, lazy-load images below the fold, and keep the hero lightweight (text and CSS-first visuals load faster than large bitmaps).
