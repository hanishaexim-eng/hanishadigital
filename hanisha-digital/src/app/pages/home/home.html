FAQ

Do you work with US startups?

Yes — we’re built for remote delivery, async collaboration, and overlapping hours for calls.

How fast is an MVP?

Many MVPs ship in 4–10 weeks depending on scope; we align weekly milestones.

How do we keep the site fast (LCP under 2.5s)?

Use WebP/AVIF, set dimensions on images, lazy-load below the fold, minimize JS, and serve from an edge CDN (e.g. Vercel). This marketing site uses CSS-first hero and text badges to avoid heavy LCP images.
